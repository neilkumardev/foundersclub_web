How it works

Six steps. Usually more than one lap.

  1. 01

    Find a need

    A real problem, for real people.

  2. 02

    Plan a small test

    The smallest thing that proves it.

  3. 03

    Build something

    Rough is fine.

  4. 04

    Test it with real people

    Not just your friends.

  5. 05

    Improve it

    Fix it, then loop back to step 3.

  6. 06

    Share or demo it

    Show what you made.

Inside the club

What actually happens here.

The core

  • Hands-on workshops

    Customer discovery, AI-assisted coding, apps and websites, marketing, business models, pitching.

  • Time to build

    Work on your project, with other builders around.

  • Feedback and check-ins

    Honest reactions, plus a board member who helps you pick your next step.

  • Teammates

    Find a team, or a cofounder.

Planned

  • Guest sessions

    Entrepreneurs and industry pros. Lineup TBA.

  • Mentor introductions

    For specific questions about your project.

  • Pitch and demo nights

    Practice on stage, building to a final showcase.

Optional

  • Competitions

    Congressional App Challenge, Blue Ocean, Diamond Challenge, and more. We’ll help you prep.

Who it’s for

Start wherever you are.

  • “I have an idea.”

    Bring it. We’ll help you test it.

  • “I want to build, but I don’t have an idea.”

    Join a team, or find a problem worth solving.

  • “I’ve never coded or started anything.”

    Perfect. You’ll learn by doing.
